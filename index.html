About VIBGOR

A school that works with parents, not around them

We keep sections small so teachers can notice who is struggling and who is ready for more. Parents get progress updates every month, not just at result time.

VIBGOR is a CBSE-affiliated school in Pune (dummy affiliation no. 1130000). Our campus has science and computer labs, a library, a playground and a quiet room for students who need a break.
